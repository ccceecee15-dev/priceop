Use `Callout` to state the one thing the reader must understand before reading the screen. Lead with a
bolded clause — `<strong>` renders copper.

```jsx
<Callout icon={<Icon name="i-info" />}>
  <strong>Setting EOL is the only trigger.</strong> The agent's first act is a plan, not a price.
</Callout>
```

This is the ONLY component in the system with a coloured left border. Do not apply that treatment to
cards — the distinction is what makes the callout read as commentary rather than content.
